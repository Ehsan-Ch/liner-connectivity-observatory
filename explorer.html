Source and interpretation

UNCTAD data via World Development Indicators. Throughput counts container traffic in twenty-foot equivalent units (TEU). Connectivity is the WDI liner shipping connectivity index with the 2004 maximum as 100. The frozen series has no connectivity observations after 2021. Throughput data for 2022–2024 are shown in the coverage counts.

Spearman's ρ measures agreement in the ordering of economies. Missing values remain missing. Changes across years can reflect changes in the matched set. A rank gap identifies an example to investigate and does not establish a cause or indicate port efficiency.
